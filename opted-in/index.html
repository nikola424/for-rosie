D5: Say This AFTER An Objection To Save The Deal

Preview Free: the 3 parts, word for word

What to say after an objection, in 3 parts

Part 1: Slow down. Don’t answer straight away. A quick answer sounds like a defence.

Part 2: Take the pressure off. “This is your call, not mine.” People relax when they don’t feel pushed.

Part 3: Ask what’s behind it. “What’s making you hesitate?” The first objection is rarely the real one.

Copy it, say it in your own words, and use it on your next call.

D6: The Most EFFECTIVE Way To Build Trust On A Call

Preview Hint: you talk less

3 ways to build trust on a call

1. Let them talk first. Ask where they are right now, and where they want to be. Then listen.

2. Tell it back. “So what I’m hearing is…” When people hear their own words, they feel understood.

3. Suggest the next step only after they’ve said what they want. Then it’s not your idea. It’s theirs.

Trust isn’t built by what you say. It’s built by how well you listen.
