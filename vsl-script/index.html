The VSL script

~9 min recorded by Rosie 13 boxes

01

The objection, spoken first

The fact that you look at me and you’re like, oh, she’s just another girl posting sales tips online. Like, just proves I marketed myself so well, and I showed up so consistently, that people know exactly what I do. So the fact that people know me as the sales girl on Instagram just proves I did my job.

02

Greeting and the elephant

Hey. It’s Rosie. Obviously, you’re here because you want clients from your content. Whether you’re getting views and no DMs, whether people go quiet the second you show your price.

And yeah. Okay. Let me address the elephant in the room. I didn’t start on Instagram. I spent 13 years in tech sales. Okay. So how am I going to be able to help you? And why am I even doing this?

This isn’t just some content hack. These are the exact systems that took me from closing multi-million dollar deals for someone else, to stuck on Instagram, to millions of views in two months and dozens of clients of my own.

03

The transferability argument

And before, hold on. Yeah. Before you disregard that, and you’re like, oh, well, you were in tech sales. How does that apply to me? Let me get one thing straight. This is not about tech sales.

The real value is the system, it’s the words, and it’s the strategy that I used to turn content into conversations and conversations into clients.

The same system that has also helped my clients. Luara went from 30,000 views a month to 436,000 in just a few weeks. Marylyn, who does business funding and credit repair, used my sales story strategy and had 30 warm leads reach out in one week. Founders just like you.

04

The pattern she has seen

Working with these founders, I’ve realized why most of them trying to get clients from Instagram actually end up quitting. Okay? It’s the same story over and over and over again.

You post every day, you chase trends, you get a few views, and when nobody buys in a month, you think, oh my god, Instagram just doesn’t work for my business, and then you lose motivation because it feels like you’re talking to nobody. That’s a lie.

05

The disqualifier

And no, I’m not going to sit here and tell you that you’re going to go viral next week, or get fully booked overnight. Okay? And honestly, if that’s what you’re looking for, you just click right off this video. That’s just not how things work.

06

The reframe

What I am about to share with you is what separates the founders who get clients from their content from the ones who post for years and never make a sale from it. Because the truth is, views mean nothing if the right people aren’t watching and nobody tells them what to do next. You could have a video with 100,000 views, and if it doesn’t bring in one conversation, it means nothing.

This applies if you’re a coach, if you run an agency, if you sell a service. It applies to every founder who wants their content to bring in clients.

07

Proof, then origin story

I’m just trying to tell you, it was not always like this. For 13 years I was in tech sales. I closed multi-million dollar deals. I was in the top 1 to 3% and I won top seller awards. Then I started my own business and posted on Instagram, and I got stuck, just like you.

So I did what I knew best. I treated my content like a sales conversation. Within two months I had millions of views and dozens of clients.

The good thing about social media is that it’s all documented online. Every post, every story, good or bad. So you can go and see all of it.

08

Turn to the viewer

But this video isn’t about me. This is for you. You’re probably stuck in that endless cycle of posting and hoping. You’re trying a new trend every week because you’re copying what you see online, from someone who has never actually sold anything.

And I can promise you, it’s not that your niche is too small. It’s not that the algorithm hates you. The problem is, to be very honest with you, you’re posting without a sales strategy. Nothing in your content leads to a conversation, and nothing in your DMs leads to a call. And you keep doing the same thing expecting different results.

Fixing this is what changed my business. It doesn’t have to take you years.

09

Three named skills

Let’s get into what actually helped me build this. It was three specific things.

Number one is positioning. People don’t buy coaching. They buy coaches. When someone lands on your profile, they need to know in five seconds who you help and why you.

Number two is content that reaches buyers. Not random views. Content that speaks to your exact buyer and ends with one clear step, like commenting a word so you can send them something. This is what nobody talks about.

Number three is the conversation. My HEARTS method. Hello, Explore, Aspire, Rewind, Tell it back, Suggest the call. This is how a DM turns into a booked call without ever feeling pushy.

10

The gap the skills don't close

But the skills aren’t the only thing you need. Because you can know all of this and still not do it, because you don’t have someone checking your content and your DMs every single week. There’s no secret. There’s no button you can click. It’s those three things and consistency over time.

11

The offer, defined by what it isn't

That’s why I built The Limitless Founder. This isn’t another course. It’s not a group chat that dies after a week. It’s not just some videos you have to figure out by yourself. It’s 100% execution. Me, working on your content, your profile and your sales conversations with you, every week.

12

The exit promise

My goal isn’t to keep you dependent on me forever. It’s to give you exactly what you need to sell with confidence, on your own. That’s what real success is.
