Your best, in depth, in one long video

75 to 105 minutes, recorded once, linked forever. What founders in your audience want most is clients from the content they already post, so the course gives them exactly that, in your words.

1. Why views don’t turn into clients. You post, you get views, and the DMs stay quiet. Or the buyer ghosts after you show the price.

2. Your story, as your first proof. 13 years in tech sales, multi-million dollar deals, then stuck on Instagram, then millions of views and dozens of clients.

3. People don’t buy coaching. They buy coaches. The profile and bio that tell a buyer in five seconds why you.

4. The content that reaches buyers, not random views. Your three content pillars.

5. Carousels that start conversations. One keyword at the end, and the DMs come to you.

6. A sneak peek inside a real session. 15 minutes from a client call (with their permission), so they see how you work before they ever book.

7. The HEARTS method. Hello, Explore, Aspire, Rewind, Tell it back, Suggest the call. The DM conversation, word for word.

8. The call. The steps before the price, the words back for the five objections, and the follow-up message.

9. In their own words. From 30K to 436K views a month, 30 warm leads in one week, a brand new account getting tens of thousands of views.

10. Their next step. Your free DM script, or a free audit with you.

The proof is woven in, never saved for the end: your story after the first lesson, the session sneak peek in the middle, and your clients’ words right before the next step.
